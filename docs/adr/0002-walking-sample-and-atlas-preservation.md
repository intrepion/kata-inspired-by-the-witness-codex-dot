# A walking route alongside the original atlas

The default entry now prioritizes the inspiration's first-person approach, tracing and physical progression. Preserve the original atlas at `/?atlas` and keep its save key untouched; the small walking route reuses the original introductory puzzles but owns separately validated progress. A compact perspective renderer with original geometric art keeps the existing dependency-free runtime; the trade-off is modest geometry and lighting rather than a large engine or campaign rewrite.
