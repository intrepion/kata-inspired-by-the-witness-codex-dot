# Stillwater: the channel walk

2026-10-15. Focused replacement for the default entry; the original fourteen-panel atlas remains at `/?atlas` with its unchanged `stillwater.v1` save. This document supersedes the 2D-only scope of the original specification for the default entry only. Baseline checkout was clean at the HEAD recorded in QA.

The design tree is settled using the user's recommended-defaults instruction: first-person walking → fixed eye height, independent mouse look, WASD, Shift → solid island bounds, trees, masonry and gates → three approachable world-mounted panels → frozen walking and a close tracing view → visible cable and gate response → final lantern overlook. Retain the original garden, tablet titles, three introductory layouts, bells, and story. No campaign expansion or imitation artwork.

The three original landing tablets teach a single rule: first connect start to end, then notice a brass seed, then apply that observation to three seeds. No rule explanation or solution appears by default. Invalid submissions pulse red, with missed seeds highlighted; a valid route glows and powers a physical gate. Any valid route is accepted. The line cannot cross itself, skip edges, or leave the grid; retracing the previous edge undoes it.

Controls (corrected interaction contract, 2026-10-15): click to capture mouse, WASD to walk, mouse to look, Shift to walk faster, click an aimed nearby panel (E is equivalent). Click the spring, trace adjacent edges, and click the endpoint; dragging and vertex clicks also work. **E/right click exits directly into active walking** and clears only the current unfinished trace. A valid solution persists and returns directly to walking. E acts once per physical press. **Escape pauses either walking or tracing**; it does not leave or clear a tablet. Resume restores the exact prior mode, active panel, path and rejection feedback. Blur/hidden-tab pauses use the same preserved-session behavior and clear held movement/drag input.

Deliberate panel entry and pause release mouse capture. Their expected lock-loss events never produce an extra pause. Leaving, solving or resuming walking requests capture within the current user gesture. Capture denial leaves exploration running with an inline click-for-mouse-look prompt; WASD and arrow look remain active. A fresh click may be required by browser security, without an intermediate pause screen. Unexpected loss of an established walking lock pauses safely. Late capture events cannot displace a resumed or active puzzle.

R retries, Backspace undoes, and arrows/Enter provide keyboard tracing. Pausing freezes the current camera and gate animation; resuming a panel continues its close view. Only completed routes are saved in `stillwater.walk.v1`; reload starts at the landing with gates restored. Paused unfinished traces live within the current page session and are preserved through pause/resume, rather than browser reload.

Public test seams authorized by the task's testable-increments requirement: existing `validate/extend`; walk `movePlayer`, `colliders`, `restoreWalk`, `solvePanel`, and `arrive`, plus `transition` for interaction sessions and `interactionKeyAction` for keyboard intent. Test concrete collision, progression, invalid route, save corruption, and cancellation semantics through their public boundaries. Browser tests cover the interaction lifecycle and visible progression; they must not set save/game state.

Acceptance: complete the three panels and walk through all three opened gates to the lantern using ordinary browser controls; observe a rejected missing-seed route, retry/reset, undo, cancellation, pointer-lock exit, focus loss, reload persistence, and preserved atlas save. Run tests, puzzle enumeration, typecheck, build, and independent standards/spec reviews. Record observed controls separately from automated coverage and tool limitations.

Research before implementation: [Jonathan Blow's developer account](https://blog.playstation.com/2013/05/23/the-witness-on-ps4-conversations-with-creators/) establishes free exploration and freedom to leave a puzzle. [Abel et al.'s original formal analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.10193) establishes simple grid paths and mandatory vertices. The [publisher's description and screenshots](https://store.steampowered.com/app/210970/The_Witness/) establish the island/panel presentation. A [detailed Steam community controls reference](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=609603354&l=latam) corroborates WASD, mouse look, left-click puzzle entry/start/end, right-click exit and Shift. This last source is community-authored, not an official manual; exact original bindings were not independently checked in the commercial executable. Browser pointer capture and Escape pausing are explicit browser adaptations.
