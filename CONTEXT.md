# Stillwater language

**Panel**: A stone tablet carrying a line puzzle.
**Trace**: A continuous path from the round spring to the square lantern along grid edges, visiting no vertex twice.
**Region**: An area of the island. Also a connected collection of panel cells bounded by the trace and outer frame; context distinguishes these meanings.
**Seed**: A diamond waypoint which the trace must visit.
**Leaf / stone**: Distinct green leaf and cream square symbols which cannot share a panel region with the other kind.
**Notch**: A triangle asking for exactly its displayed number of surrounding cell edges to be traced.
**Memory**: A story fragment revealed by restoring a panel.
**Restoration**: A successful submission that remains recorded on revisits.
